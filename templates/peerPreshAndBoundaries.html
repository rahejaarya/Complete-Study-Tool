Peer Pressure & Boundaries

Practical ways to speak up for what feels right to you

YOU CAN TAKE TIME TO DECIDE

A clear response is enough

You do not have to agree right away or explain yourself. Give yourself a moment, then choose the words that feel comfortable and safe.

  • Pause: Take a breath and give yourself a moment before answering.
  • Be clear: Try, “No thanks, that is not for me.”
  • Repeat: Calmly repeat your answer if someone keeps asking.
  • Make an exit: Move toward a safe place or a person you trust if you feel pressured.
  • Get support: Talk with a trusted adult, counselor, or friend if you feel unsafe.
