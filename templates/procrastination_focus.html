START SMALL

You do not need to feel motivated to begin.

When a task feels huge, your brain treats it like a threat. Shrink the job until it feels safe enough to start.

What helps when procrastination hits?

Try a few strategies that make starting easier, not harder.

🎯

Make the first step tiny

Write only 1 sentence, open the worksheet, or set a 5-minute timer. Progress starts before motivation does.

→
🧩

Break it into pieces

Instead of “study for the test,” try “review chapter 2 questions 1–5” or “make a 3-bullet outline.”

→
🌿

Lower the pressure

Your goal is to begin, not to be perfect. A rough draft, rough notes, or partial answer still counts as momentum.

→
💡

Use a reset ritual

Put your phone away, drink water, take three slow breaths, and set a focused timer before you start.
